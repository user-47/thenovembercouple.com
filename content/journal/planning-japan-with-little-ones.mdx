Two weeks, two cities, two small travellers. Here's the thinking behind [our itinerary](/trips/japan) — the rules we kept coming back to.

## One big thing a day

Each day has one anchor — Disneyland, teamLab, the ninja village on Awaji — and everything else is optional. If the anchor goes well, the day is a win.

## Go early, rest midday

Kyoto's temples are calm at 8:00 and chaos by 10:30. We plan the famous sights for first thing, then build in a lunch-and-nap reset before an easy afternoon.

## Book the unmissables first

Some tickets go on sale on a fixed day at a fixed minute. We booked those before anything else — the [booking calendar](/journal/japan-booking-calendar) has every date we planned around.

## Make the transfers part of the fun

A reserved row on the Shinkansen, an ekiben lunch, and Mt Fuji appearing on the right about forty minutes out of Tokyo. Forward the big suitcases and the bullet train becomes a highlight, not a chore.

## End with a taste of home

Our last night is Nigerian food in Tokyo — jollof and egusi at Esogie. A small ritual to close a big trip.
